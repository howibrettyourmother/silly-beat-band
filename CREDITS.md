## Recorded sounds (all CC0 1.0 / public domain, from Freesound.org)
Each clip's license was checked on its Freesound page. Clips were trimmed to the hit, faded, peak-normalized to -1 dBFS, and saved as small mono MP3s (64-96 kbps). The game also skips any leading silence when it loads each sample so hits land on the beat. **Sounds were chosen by analysis (onset, length, loudness, pitch), not by listening.**
